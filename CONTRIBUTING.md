# Contributing visual assets

We welcome careful improvements, accessible exports, and practical variants.

Please include:

1. the intended surface and minimum useful size;
2. an editable source when one exists;
3. a transparent PNG export for raster work;
4. provenance and license notes for every outside input;
5. a manifest hash for the final artifact.

Keep the graduate-duck character recognizable, spell `OpenKaggle` exactly, and
avoid designs that could be confused with an official Kaggle or Google mark.
Do not add third-party fonts, illustrations, or templates without a documented
redistribution right.
